Clip Finder

Clip Finder is a personal tool that turns highlights from Twitch streams I'm authorized to clip (my own channel or paid clipping campaigns) into short vertical videos, and posts them to my own TikTok and YouTube accounts.

For TikTok, every post is reviewed and confirmed by me in the app: I see my account, choose who can see the video, set comments/Duet/Stitch, disclose paid content when applicable, and agree to TikTok's terms before anything is sent.
