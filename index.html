Clip Finder · ChadCuts

Clip Finder is a personal tool that turns highlights from Twitch streams I'm authorized to clip (my own channel or paid clipping campaigns that invite clippers) into short vertical videos, and posts them to my own TikTok and YouTube accounts (@chadcuts1).

▶ YouTube Uploads to YouTube use YouTube API Services; see the YouTube Terms of Service and Google Privacy Policy.

For TikTok, every post is reviewed and confirmed by me in the app: I see my account, choose who can see the video, set comments/Duet/Stitch, disclose paid content when applicable, and agree to TikTok's terms before anything is sent.
