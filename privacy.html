Privacy policy

Last updated: November 19, 2026. Clip Finder (used for the ChadCuts channels) runs on its owner's own computer and is used only by its owner, Jackson Petty. Contact: jackson.lee.petty@gmail.com

YouTube API Services

Clip Finder uses YouTube API Services to upload videos to the owner's own YouTube channel and to check their processing status. By using Clip Finder you agree to be bound by the YouTube Terms of Service. YouTube data is handled according to the Google Privacy Policy.

  • YouTube data accessed: the connected channel's name and avatar (youtube.readonly), permission to upload videos (youtube.upload), and the IDs and processing status of videos this app uploaded.
  • Use: only to show which channel is connected, upload the clips the owner approved or scheduled, and show whether each upload succeeded.
  • Storage: OAuth tokens are stored encrypted on the owner's computer. Video IDs and statuses are stored in the app's local database. Nothing is stored on any server we run.
  • Sharing: YouTube data is never sold or shared with third parties.
  • Deletion and revocation: clicking "Disconnect" in the app revokes the app's access with Google and deletes the stored tokens and channel info immediately. You can also revoke access at any time on the Google security settings page. The local record of uploads this app made (video ID, title, status) stays on the owner's computer as posting history until the owner deletes it or the app's data folder.

TikTok and Twitch

  • TikTok: basic account info (display name, avatar) and permission to post videos the owner approves. Disconnecting revokes access and deletes the tokens; access can also be revoked in TikTok's settings.
  • Twitch: public chat activity counts and the live video of streams the owner is authorized to clip. Chat message text is not kept long-term.

Cookies

The app does not use cookies or tracking for advertising.
