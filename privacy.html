Privacy policy

Clip Finder runs on its owner's own computer and is used only by its owner.

  • What it accesses: the owner's TikTok account basic info (display name, avatar) and permission to post videos the owner approves; the owner's YouTube channel info and permission to upload; public Twitch chat activity counts.
  • Storage: access tokens are stored encrypted on the owner's computer. Chat message text is not stored.
  • Sharing: no data is sold or shared with third parties. Data is sent only to TikTok, YouTube and Twitch to perform actions the owner requested.
  • Deletion: disconnecting an account in the app deletes its tokens. Access can also be revoked in TikTok, Google and Twitch account settings at any time.
